About Me

        
Hey! I'm Lucas, born April 30, 2004, a Brazilian software developer who loves turning ideas into real projects, especially when they involve creativity, technology, and a bit of experimentation.

        
I'm currently working as a Developer at A30x1, developing add-ons and gameplay experiences for the Minecraft Bedrock Marketplace.

        
My interests go beyond game development. I enjoy exploring software engineering, cryptography, embedded systems, artificial intelligence, and automation. Whether I'm building an encrypted password manager, experimenting with ESP32 microcontrollers, or developing local AI assistants, I'm always looking for something new to create.

        
I primarily work with TypeScript, JavaScript, and Node.js, but I'm constantly learning new technologies and expanding my skills.
